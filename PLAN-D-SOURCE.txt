PLAN D — 18X FOUNDATION
A 13-week foundation program for the road to SOCOM Athlete Hell Day and an 18X contract.
Overview
This is a 13-week, 6-day-per-week foundation program built for one athlete: 147 pounds, average build, 7 max pull-ups on Day
0, training toward a SOCOM Athlete Hell Day event (October–December 2026, Florida) with the goal of performing well enough
in front of active-duty Special Forces cadre to earn an 18X contract conversation. It begins with a 2-mile time trial in Week 1
and ends with a full retest in Week 13. The week rotates through four runs, three strength sessions, near-daily pull-up volume,
pool work from Week 6, and one full rest day. Rucking is locked until the 2-mile run drops under 16:00 — and that lock is the
single most important design decision in the program.
Introduction
Everyone who starts down this road wants to put the ruck on first. Don't. Selection is won by the guys who show up durable —
the ones whose feet, shins, and knees survived their own preparation. Cadre screen hardest on run times, ruck times, and the
simple refusal to quit. Nobody at selection asks how much you bench.
This program builds you in the right order: aerobic engine first, tissue durability second, strength that supports the first two, and
only then load carriage. Some sessions will feel too easy. That is the program working. The only real failure mode over 13
weeks is injury, and injury almost always comes from enthusiasm, not laziness.
The Philosophy — Five Rules
1. Running is the priority. Cadre screen run and ruck times hardest. Every other session exists to support the runs.
2. Rucking is gated. No rucks until the 2-mile is under 16:00 (retests in Weeks 5, 9, 13). Rucking rides on run fitness; done too
early it breaks shins and steals weeks.
3. Consistency beats heroics. A merely-good week completed beats a perfect week abandoned. If life wrecks a day, slide the
plan a day — never cram two sessions into one.
4. Log four numbers. Friday: 2-minute push-ups, 2-minute sit-ups, fasted bodyweight. Saturday: run times. What gets
measured gets improved.
5. Never skip the warm-up. A Special Forces team leader tore a bicep tendon skipping warm-ups at 40 and carried the injury
for years. Every session starts with the standard warm-up in Part 3. No exceptions, no matter how short on time.

If You Must Drop Something
Fatigue, work, life — drop in this order: accessory lifts → ruck extras → never the runs or pull-up days. One full skipped day
beats a garbage week. Slide the start date rather than skipping content.
The Sessions — Every Workout Explained
Read Part 2 for what to do each day; read this part for exactly how. Each session has a goal, a warm-up, execution, and a
"done when."
Standard Warm-Ups
• RUN WARM-UP (5 min): brisk walk 2 min → leg swings 10/leg front + side → 20 walking lunges → 10 bodyweight squats →
2 min easy jog.
• LIFT WARM-UP (5 min): arm circles → band pull-aparts x15 → scap pull-ups x8 (hang, pull shoulder blades down without
bending arms) → 10 slow push-ups → 1 light set of the day's first lift.
• Stretch AFTER training, when muscles are warm. Never cold-stretch before.

2-Mile Time Trial (Weeks 1 / 5 / 9 / 13)
Protocol: full run warm-up + 5 min easy jog + 3 x 20s pickups, rest 3 min. Then 2 miles ALL OUT on a flat measured course
(track = 8 laps, or GPS out-and-back). Same course every retest. Pacing: first mile "strong but scared," second mile empty the
tank. Going out too fast is the #1 way to blow it. Log the time the same day.
Ruck (Saturday post-unlock, odd weeks — replaces the tempo)
Goal: time under load, WALKING only. Jogging with a ruck is how knees die. Target ~15–17 min/mile, hip-driven stride.
• Load: Week 7 = 20 lb → +5 lb per ruck → hold at 35 lb. Stair-step weight OR duration, never both.
• Pack: weight HIGH and TIGHT against the upper back (towel-wrap the plates), every strap cinched, sternum strap clipped. A
swinging load doubles the fatigue.
• Feet: wool socks, broken-in boots. FIRST hot spot = stop immediately, tape or moleskin. A blister today costs two weeks.
• The pace math: 20:00/mi is casual. 15:00/mi is the standard target. Under 14:00/mi walking is the SFAS-competitive zone —
built over months, not weeks.

Work Capacity (Friday)
Goal: strength-endurance under fatigue — the closest thing to selection in the week. Purposeful pace, never sloppy.
1. DB deadlifts 4 x 6–8 (heaviest pair) — hinge, flat back, stand tall. Rest 2 min. This part is strength, not conditioning.
2. Then 3–4 rounds, minimal rest: farmer carry 50m (tall posture) → 10 burpees → 15 ruck swings (grab the straps; hips do
the work, arms are ropes) → 10 push-ups → 20m bear crawl (knees 1 inch off the floor).
Target: all rounds under 25 minutes. Log the time. Beat it next week.
Friday PT Test (do FIRST, fresh)
2-min max push-ups → rest 5 min → 2-min max sit-ups. Push-ups full range (chest low, full lockout), rest only in the up-plank.
Sit-ups feet anchored, shoulder blades touch down, all the way up. Cheat reps steal your own data. Log both numbers plus
the fasted weigh-in.
The Pull-Up Block (Monday + Friday)
Goal: ~30 total CLEAN reps. This is Littlestone doctrine: make every rep count.
• Sets of 3–5 (10x3 or 6x5), rest 60–90s. Stop each set 1–2 reps before failure — grinding ugly reps teaches bad patterns.
• Standard: dead hang at the bottom (arms straight), chin clearly over — chest-to-bar is the gold standard. No kip, no swing,
no cheat.
• Grips: rotate wide overhand / shoulder-width overhand / chin-up across sets. Prioritize wide — it loads the big back muscles
and spares the biceps. If a grip hurts a joint, change grips.
• Can't get 3 clean? Band over the bar, foot in the loop — band reps count. Use the lightest band allowing 5 perfect reps;
graduate to thinner bands, then none.
• Out of reps? Negatives: jump to the top, lower for 5 seconds. These build full reps faster than grinding.

Pool (Wednesday, Week 6+)
Goal: water comfort — Hell Day has pool evolutions. Comfort beats speed. 10–15 min easy laps (rest at the wall freely) → 2 x
2 min treading water (slow scissor kicks, sculling hands, stay calm) → 10 controlled bobs: exhale slowly UNDERWATER
through the nose, surface, inhale, repeat. That calm-under-water reflex is exactly what cadre look for.
Rest Day (Sunday)
Goal: adaptation — the week only lands if you recover from it. Sleep 8+, hit the calorie numbers, easy 20–30 min walk if antsy.
NO bonus workouts; extra work today steals from Tuesday. Sunday is also batch-cook day (Part 5).
Exercise Form Guide
Setup, execution, and the mistake that ruins each movement. When in doubt, film yourself on your phone and compare against
the cues.
Push-Up Family
• Standard: hands under shoulders, body one straight line, glutes squeezed. Chest to the floor, elbows ~45° from the sides
(not flared to 90°), full lockout. Hips sag or pike = the rep doesn't count.
• Feet-elevated: feet on a chair (12–20"), same rules — shifts load to shoulders/upper chest.
• Deficit: hands on plates/books so the chest travels BELOW hand level. Bigger range = harder.
• Tempo: 3-second lower, 1-second pause at the bottom, explode up.
• Pike: hips high in an inverted V, lower the top of the head to the floor between the hands — the overhead-press substitute.
Dumbbell Movements
• DB deadlift: hips push BACK (not down), flat back, DBs slide along the legs to mid-shin, drive through the heels, squeeze
glutes tall. The bend comes from the hips, never the spine. If the lower back rounds, stop the descent higher.
• Farmer carry: heaviest pair, stand tall, shoulders back and down, normal steps. Grip gives out? That IS the stimulus — set
down, shake out, continue.
• Row / press / RDL / goblet: cues in the Part 3 tables.
Core & Circuit Pieces
• Sit-up: knees bent, hands crossed on chest or fingertips at temples — never yanking the neck. Shoulder blades touch down,
torso all the way up.
• Flutter kicks (4-count): hands under tailbone, heels 6" off the floor, small alternating kicks; right-left-right = "one." Lower
back stays glued down — if it arches, raise the legs.
• Burpee: squat, hands down, kick back, one full push-up (chest to floor), snap in, jump with hands overhead. Sloppy burpees
train sloppiness.
• Ruck swing: both hands on the top straps, hinge (flat back), swing between the legs, snap the hips forward to chest height.
It's a hip snap, not an arm lift — the DB deadlift pattern made explosive.
• Bear crawl: hands and feet, knees an inch off the ground, back flat enough to balance a cup of water, opposite hand/foot
together.
• Ruck squat: ruck on, feet shoulder-width, hips back and down to parallel, knees tracking over toes, head up.
Progression Rule (all calisthenics)
When you clear the top of a rep range on every set, make the movement harder — elevate the feet, add the ruck, slow the
tempo, or advance the variation. Harder beats more reps past ~15.
Optional Later Buy
A ~60 lb sandbag ($40 duffel + play sand) for awkward-object work before the event — sandbag-to-shoulder mimics log PT
(deadlift to the lap, hug, one aggressive extension up and over the shoulder, alternate sides, back flat on every pickup). Not
needed for the base block.

Slow cooker — Beef Chili (5 svgs, ~550 cal / 42g P): brown 2 lb 90/10 beef first (don't skip — it's the flavor), then cooker with

Skillet — Honey Garlic Chicken (4 svgs, ~540 cal / 42g P): sauce = ¼ cup honey + ¼ cup soy + 4 cloves garlic. Cube + sear
1.5 lb thighs, pour sauce, simmer 4–5 min until it coats (pull before it burns). Rice + steamed broccoli.
Skillet — Korean Beef Bowls (4 svgs, ~530 cal / 40g P): brown 1.5 lb 90/10 beef, drain. Sauce in-pan: ¼ cup soy + 2 tbsp
brown sugar + 1 tbsp sesame oil + garlic + ginger + sriracha. Simmer 3 min. Over rice + cucumber.
Flavor rotation cheat code: same protein + rice base, swap the finisher jar — salsa+cheese / soy+honey+garlic / tikka sauce
/ BBQ+ranch / pesto+parmesan. One jar changes the week.

Day-Type Adjustments
• TUE & SAT (hard runs): +1 carb serving the meal before (extra rice, bagel, or banana).
• FRI: weigh in fasted — after bathroom, before coffee/food. Log with PT numbers.
• SUN: no workout = no shake = ~2,150 cal automatically. Protein stays covered.
• Travel/site days: jerky + shake + banana + chocolate milk from any gas station ≈ 600 cal / 50g P. Missed a meal? Don't
double up — hit protein snacks and move on. The week average is what counts.
• Alcohol: costs recovery. If it happens, keep it off Friday/Saturday nights — protect the big sessions and sleep.
